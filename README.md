# Zen Garden

A small pixel-art sand garden for the browser. Rake patterns into the sand, place and move stones, and rake rings around them.

- **Rake (1):** drag to draw grooves that follow your hand
- **Stone (2):** click to place a stone, drag one to move it, scroll to change the size, right-click to remove it
- **Smooth (3):** drag to flatten the sand
- **Rings (4):** click a stone to rake circles around it, or click open sand for a ripple
- **Undo:** Ctrl+Z
- **Settings (S):** sand color, time of day (day, dusk, night), rake width, groove depth, stone size, moss, volume, and buttons to rake everything straight, shuffle the stones, or clear them
- **Sound (M)** and **full screen (Enter)**

Your garden is saved in your browser. Everything is one file, `index.html`, with no build step.
